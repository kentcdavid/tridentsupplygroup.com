Who We Are

Built on Integrity.
Driven by Results.

Trident Supply Group LLC is a full-service supply and distribution company committed to helping businesses source the materials, equipment, and consumables they need — on time and on budget.

We leverage a robust supplier network and deep industry knowledge to deliver competitive pricing, flexible fulfillment, and the personalized service that larger suppliers simply can't match. Whether you need a single critical component or ongoing procurement support, we're ready.

Our name says it all — three prongs, one purpose: to connect you with the right products, the right partners, and the right price, every time.
